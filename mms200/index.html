MMS 200 Resource Hub

Writing guidelines and templates for the MMS 200 Special Project manuscript. Choose the track that matches your project, read its guidelines first, then make a copy of the matching template to start writing.

Manuscript templates are provided in LaTeX. LaTeX preserves the required formatting (headings, numbering, table of contents, and citations) no matter how often the document is edited, unlike Word files, which tend to break as content changes.
